Voice & Tone

Brand Voice

Warm & Direct All channels
Short sentences. Say the real thing. No corporate language, no hollow filler. Warmth earns trust — it doesn't need to be performed.
"Stories your kids will ask for every night." — not — "Delivering premium AI-powered narrative experiences for discerning families."
Respectful Authority on Faith & Culture Community content
Communities lead. We never approximate faith from outside. Language is accurate, never appropriating. When in doubt, defer to the community ambassador's voice.
"Reviewed by our Muslim community ambassador" — not — "Islamic-inspired content for diverse families."
Calm at Bedtime In-app copy
The app voice slows down after 7pm. In-app micro-copy is quieter, gentler. No urgency. No notifications phrased as demands. The UI knows it's bedtime.
"Sleep timer is on. Goodnight. 🌙" — not — "Don't forget to rate tonight's story!"
Real Parent Voice Social / outreach
Deepti's channel voice — genuine, first-person, lived experience. "My daughter asked for it again" lands harder than any product claim. Lean on it without over-directing it.
"She actually asked me to play it again at 10pm 😂 — it was the Eid one." — authentic, not scripted.

Words we use

Audio Direction

Narration & Sound Design

Primary Narrator Voice

The Trusted Aunt / Uncle

Warmth: High — like someone who knows your child's name
Pace: 110–130 wpm (slightly slower than conversation)
Pitch: Mid-low. Never high or performative
Accent: Neutral North American, clearly articulated
Gender: One male, one female option per series
Age feel: 30s–40s, parent or older sibling energy

Community Narrators

Native Authenticity Rule

For belief-specific series, narrators are native community members — fluent in cultural cadence, festival pronunciation, and tone. The Eid series narrator should be someone who celebrates Eid. This is non-negotiable.

Bilingual series (Latino): Spanish-dominant delivery with English phrasing natural, not translated-feeling.

Sound Design — Ambient Layer

Bedtime Soundscape

Base layer: Soft brown noise or gentle rain, -18 to -22 dBFS
Mid layer: Story-specific ambience (forest: crickets + leaves; desert: distant wind; home: fireplace crackle) at -24 dBFS
Accent layer: Sparse musical motifs — solo piano or oud — triggered at scene changes, 2–4 second phrases only

Rule: Audio must never compete with narration. If in doubt, lower it.

Narration Delivery Rules

Breathing: Audible soft breaths are allowed — they signal presence
Pauses: 0.5–1s pause after each scene transition
Sentence endings: Drop slightly in pitch — signals safety, not urgency
Character voices: Subtle shifts only. No exaggerated cartoon voices.
Sleep mode: After ch. 2, pace slows 10–15%, volume fades gently
End phrase: Every story ends with "Goodnight, [child's name]." narrated softly.

Musical Identity

Instrument palette

Solo piano, classical oud/sitar (for cultural series), soft marimba, bowed string (cello). No synth pads. No percussion.

Tempo & key

48–72 BPM. Major keys for joyful episodes, natural minor for reflective ones. Avoid minor 2nds — they signal tension, not rest.

UI sounds

Single soft chime (C5, 80ms decay) for story start. No error sounds. Tap interactions: haptic only, no audio. Sleep timer end: fade to silence over 10s.

Motion & Interaction

Animation Principles

Slow everything down by 20% in Sleep Mode
All transitions extend, taps respond sluggishly-on-purpose. The UI reinforces the ritual of winding down.
duration × 1.2, easing: ease-in-out
Story card entrance — float up
Cards drift upward softly on load, staggered by 80ms per card. Like pages turning in a book left on the bed.
translateY(12px → 0), opacity 0→1, 400ms, stagger 80ms
Story open — slow fade, not slide
Opening a story is not navigation — it's entering a world. Fade in (no directional slide) over 500ms.
opacity 0→1, 500ms, ease-in-out. No translateX.
Ambient star particles on hero
3–5 floating dot particles on the dark hero section — slow, random drift. Barely noticeable, but felt. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
CSS: @keyframes float, 8–14s duration, random offset
No bounce, no spring, no playful easing
This is bedtime, not a toy. Bouncy animations signal play — the opposite of wind-down. All easing: ease-in-out or cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1).
Never: spring(), bounce, elastic

Do / Don't

Key Design Rules

✓ Do
Use Lora for any headline that carries emotional weight
✗ Don't
Use DM Sans for hero headlines — it reads corporate
✓ Do
Dark backgrounds for immersive story screens (mirrors bedtime)
✗ Don't
Use pure white (#FFFFFF) for in-app reading — too harsh at night
✓ Do
Name community content by who made it: "Our Sikh community series"
✗ Don't
Say "inspired by" or "celebrates" — vague language reads as appropriation
✓ Do
Let amber (#E8A66B) be a true accent — used sparingly for warmth moments
✗ Don't
Use amber for all CTAs — violet (#7B5FC0) is the interactive color
