## What We're Building

We're adding a "My Voice" feature to the Rainbow Kindergarten episodes on My Sleepy Tale. The idea: the AI narrator still tells the full story, but at key moments — 3 per episode — the child's OWN voice drops in with a word or phrase they recorded about what they remember from that experience.

So instead of just hearing a narrator say "And they found hexagons at the park," a child hears THEIR voice saying "Hexagons! They were everywhere!" right inside the story. It's their memory, in their words, woven into the bedtime experience.

## Three Phases — Aligned to How Kids Grow

We've thought about this through the lens of the JK → SK → Grade 1 progression:

**Phase 1 — JK (Word Collectors)**
Kids record single words or 2-word bursts: "Hexagons!" / "Big turtle!" / "It was cold!"
This builds vocabulary recall, phonological awareness, and the confidence that comes from hearing your own voice in something "real."

**Phase 2 — SK (Sentence Builders)**
Kids record short sentences: "I found a hexagon on the ground" / "The turtle was sitting on a rock"
This develops oral fluency, sequencing (what happened first, next, last), and emotional vocabulary.

**Phase 3 — Grade 1 (Story Makers)**
Kids record multi-sentence narratives with opinions: "We went to Brick Works and I saw a turtle. It was sharing the rock. I think turtles are nice because they share."
This strengthens story composition, critical thinking, and true ownership of learning.

The beauty is that even JK kids get to participate — a single excited "Hexagons!" is just as powerful in the story as a full paragraph. Every child's contribution fits.

## What's Already Built

The recording feature is live in the app right now for all 3 Rainbow Kindergarten episodes. Each episode has 3 age-appropriate prompts. Parents can record their child's voice at home in under a minute. It's completely optional — the story plays beautifully with or without the recordings.
