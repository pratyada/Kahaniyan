## What is My Sleepy Tale?

An audio-first ed-tech platform where children explore their culture, learn strong values, and create their own stories about the world around them. No screens. No animations. Just a warm voice, a cozy blanket, and a story with your child's name in it.

**Live:** [mysleepytale.com](https://mysleepytale.com)

## Platform Features

### For Children
- **Personalized Stories** — child's name woven into every story
- **Audio-Only Playback** — no screen distractions, dark player UI
- **Sleep Mode** — screen goes completely black, audio plays with minimal controls (play/pause/next). Auto-plays next story. Parent-designed for bedtime.
- **Multilingual** — listen to the same story in 9 languages
- **Photo Gallery** — swipeable photos during story playback
- **Badges & Streaks** — 23+ unlockable badges, daily listening streaks
- **Radio** — 20 global stations (Kids-Friendly lullabies + General ambient)

### For Parents
- **Family Profiles** — up to 10 child profiles (Family tier)
- **Belief Filtering** — stories filtered by family's cultural tradition
- **Voice Cloning** — record your own voice for narration (Pro+)
- **Feedback Button** — quick feedback during playback
- **Share Cards** — beautiful shareable story cards for social media

### For Creators
- **Story Submission** — community writers submit stories for review
- **Credit System** — earn credits for approved stories, plays, shares
- **Creator Tiers** — Storyteller → Keeper → Elder → Guardian
- **Leaderboard** — top creators ranked by tradition
- **Private Series** — create family-only series with invited contributors

### For the Founder (Founder Hub)
- **Content Publisher** — create + publish episodes, series, stories, blogs from one place
- **Marketing Agents** — Reddit automation, Instagram creative generator
- **Content Pipeline** — AI-powered multi-agent content generation
- **Outreach** — branded email campaigns to partners

## Brand Guidelines

- **Voice:** Warm, gentle, inclusive. Never salesy. Always parent-to-parent.
- **Terminology:** Use "platform" or "web space" — never "app"
- **Cultural sensitivity:** Always respectful. Never depict God or any Prophet in images (Islamic). Never show Guru faces (Sikh) — use symbols, gurdwara, light instead.
- **Prophet Mohamed:** Always use "peace be upon him" suffix
- **Kids language:** Soft, positive words only. No "grabbed", "exploded", "scary"
- **Colors:** Gold (#f0a500) on dark navy (#0a0a0f). Day mode: Purple (#6B4FA8) on cream (#FDF8F0)
