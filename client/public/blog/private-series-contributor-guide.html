Private Series &
Contributor Flow

Create private bedtime story series for your family. Share them with loved ones. Let anyone you trust contribute their own episodes. Together, build a story collection that is truly yours.

Public vs Personal

You can now create two types of series on My Sleepy Tale:

🌍 Public Series

  • Visible to everyone worldwide
  • Admin review before publishing
  • Listed in Search and Series tab
  • Great for community stories

🔒 Personal Series

  • Only you and shared people can see
  • Published instantly, no review
  • Perfect for family memories
  • Shared users can contribute episodes

Think of it this way

A private series is a shared family journal in audio form. A grandmother in India starts a series about family traditions. A cousin in Canada adds an episode about their first snow day. A friend in the US contributes their favourite memory. Everyone listens at bedtime.

Step by Step

1

"Shared with me" on the Homepage

When someone shares a private series with you, a new "Shared with me" tab appears on your homepage, right next to Story and Series. You see the series title, creator's name, and all episode cards.

Shared User
2

Open the Series & Contribute

Tap into the series to see all episodes. Below the episode cards, find the "+ Contribute an episode to this series" button. Write a title, story text (full story or quick bullet points), and upload multiple photos.

Contributor
3

Submit for Review

Hit "Submit for review" and see a confirmation. The series owner will be notified and can review your submission.

Contributor
4

Owner Sees Pending Contributions

The owner opens their series and sees "Pending Contributions" — a greyed-out card with "PENDING" badge, contributor's name, word count, and date.

Series Owner
5

Review, Edit & Publish

The owner taps the pending card to open the review panel. They see submitted photos, the original text, and editable fields for title and body. Polish the story, then tap "Publish as Episode" or "Reject" with feedback.

Series Owner
6

Published — Everyone Can Listen

The new episode appears alongside existing ones. The owner can further enhance it using Edit mode: upload more photos, edit text, and generate fresh AI audio narration — all in one tap.

Published

Two Roles, One Story

Series Owner

  • Creates and names the series
  • Adds the initial episodes
  • Shares with people by email
  • Reviews and edits contributions
  • Uploads images, generates audio
  • Full creative control

Contributor

  • Sees series in "Shared with me"
  • Listens to all published episodes
  • Submits episodes (text + photos)
  • Quick pointers or full stories
  • Owner polishes before publishing
  • No technical skills needed

Who Is This For?

Families

  • Let grandparents, aunts, cousins contribute

Built with Love

This feature was born from a real conversation. Deepti Ramaul and Sravanthi Somalaraju came up with the idea of turning family memories into private bedtime stories that everyone could contribute to. Deepti created the first series — "Veda's Mountain Love — Denver Stories 2022-23". Sravanthi and Prateek tested the full flow end to end. Thank you both for believing in this idea.
