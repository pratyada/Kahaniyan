Private Series & Contributor Flow

Create private bedtime story series for your family. Share them with loved ones. Let anyone you trust contribute their own episodes. Together, you build a story collection that is truly yours.

What Are Private Series?

On My Sleepy Tale, you can now create two types of series:

Public Series

  • Visible to all My Sleepy Tale users worldwide
  • Goes through admin review before publishing
  • Listed in Search, Series tab, and Creator profiles
  • Great for sharing cultural stories with the community

Private Series

  • Only visible to you and people you share with
  • Published instantly, no review needed
  • Perfect for family memories, personal stories
  • Shared users can listen and contribute episodes

Think of a private series as a shared family journal in audio form. A grandmother in India can start a series about family traditions. A cousin in Canada can add an episode about their first snow day. A friend in the US can contribute their favourite childhood memory. Everyone who is shared can listen to the whole collection at bedtime.

How It Works — The Complete Flow

The Journey of a Private Series

1

Owner Creates a Private Series

Go to Create, choose "Series", toggle visibility to "Personal", add episodes with story text and images. Published instantly.

|
2

Owner Shares with Family & Friends

From the series card in "My Creations", tap "+ Share with someone" and enter their email. They are instantly added.

|
3

Shared User Sees "Shared with me" Tab

On their homepage, a new tab appears: "Shared with me (1)". They see the series with all episodes, ready to listen.

|
4

Contributor Adds an Episode

Inside the series, they tap "+ Contribute an episode". They write a title, story text (or quick bullet points), and upload photos. Then submit for review.

|
5

Owner Reviews the Submission

The owner sees a greyed-out "Pending" card. They tap to review: see the original text, submitted photos, edit the story, polish the writing.

|
6

Owner Publishes the Episode

One tap on "Publish as Episode" and it becomes the next episode in the series. The owner can then add images and generate AI audio narration.

|
7

Everyone Sees the New Episode

All shared users (and the owner) instantly see the new episode in the series. Ready to listen at bedtime.

Step-by-Step with Screenshots

Step 1: "Shared with me" on the Homepage

When someone shares a private series with you, a new "Shared with me" tab appears on your homepage, right next to Story and Series. You see the series title, the creator's name, and all episode cards — ready to play.

Step 2: Open the Series & Contribute

Tap into the series to see all episodes. Below the episode cards, you will find the "+ Contribute an episode to this series" button. This is your invitation to add your own story to the collection.

Step 3: Write & Submit Your Episode

Write your episode title and story text. You can write a full story or just quick bullet points of memories — the series owner can polish it later. You can also upload multiple photos to go with your episode. All images are automatically compressed for fast loading.

Hit "Submit for review" and you will see a confirmation that your episode has been submitted. The series owner will be notified.

Step 4: Owner Sees Pending Contributions

The series owner opens their series and sees a new section: "Pending Contributions". The submitted episode appears as a greyed-out card with a "PENDING" badge, showing the contributor's name, word count, and submission date.

Step 5: Owner Reviews, Edits & Publishes

The owner taps the pending card to open the review panel. Here they can see:

  • Submitted photos — all images the contributor uploaded, displayed as a scrollable gallery
  • Original submission — the raw text exactly as the contributor wrote it
  • Editable title and body — the owner can rewrite, polish, expand, or restructure the story as needed

When ready, the owner taps "Publish as Episode" to add it to the series. Or "Reject" with optional feedback if changes are needed.

Step 6: Published — Everyone Can Listen

After publishing, the new episode appears alongside the existing ones. In this example, the series now has 3 episodes — two by the original creator and one contributed by a family member. The hero collage at the top updates with images from all episodes.

The owner can further enhance the published episode using Edit mode: upload more photos, edit the story text, and generate fresh AI audio narration — all in one tap.

Two Roles, One Story

Series Owner

  • Creates and names the series
  • Adds the initial episodes
  • Shares with people by email
  • Reviews and edits contributions
  • Publishes or rejects submissions
  • Uploads images and generates audio
  • Full creative control

Contributor

  • Sees shared series on their homepage
  • Listens to all published episodes
  • Submits new episodes (text + photos)
  • Can write full stories or quick bullet points
  • Owner polishes before publishing
  • No technical skills needed
  • Just share your memories

Built with Love — Thank You

This feature was born from a real conversation. Deepti Ramaul and Sravanthi Somalaraju came up with the idea of turning family memories into private bedtime stories that everyone could contribute to — not just one person writing, but the whole family adding their own chapters.

Deepti created the first private series — "Veda's Mountain Love — Denver Stories 2022-23" — documenting her family's adventure moving from Toronto to Colorado. Sravanthi and Prateek tested the full contributor flow end to end, finding bugs, suggesting improvements, and making sure it felt natural for real families.

Thank you, Deepti and Sravanthi, for believing in this idea and helping us build it right. This feature exists because of you.
