Where Each App Wins

Calm Kids — Best for generic sleep sounds

Calm Kids excels at ambient sleep content: rain sounds, white noise, and beautifully produced sleep stories read by celebrities. If your only goal is "make my child fall asleep fast" without caring about values or learning — Calm is fine.

Weakness: Zero personalization. Zero cultural awareness. Every child hears the same story. $14.99/mo for a glorified podcast.

Moshi Sleep — Best for character play

Moshi has cute original characters that kids love. The stories are entertaining and well-produced. Good for ages 3-6.

Weakness: Stories are entertainment, not education. No moral framework. Kids age out quickly. No cultural representation.

Headspace for Kids — Best for meditation

If you want guided meditation for kids, Headspace is excellent. Their "wind-down" content helps anxious children.

Weakness: It's a meditation app with stories added as an afterthought. Limited story library. Secular only — doesn't serve faith-based families.
