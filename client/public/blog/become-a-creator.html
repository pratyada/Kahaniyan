How It Works

1

Sign up as a Creator

Create your account on My Sleepy Tale. Go to Settings → "Become a Creator." Pick your tradition.

2

Write a story from your tradition

A moral story (5-10 minutes read) that teaches one value: kindness, courage, honesty, patience, sharing, respect, or forgiveness. Can be from scripture, folk tradition, or your own family lore.

3

Submit for review

Our team reads every submission. If it's respectful, well-written, and teaches a clear value — it gets approved within 48 hours.

4

We narrate it with AI voice + generate cover art

Your story gets a professional audio narration and beautiful cover image. It appears in the app for all families of that tradition.

5

Earn credits for every view

Every time a family plays your story, you earn 1 credit. Credits can be redeemed for free Pro subscription, gift cards, or donated to charity.

Story Guidelines

  • ✓ Must teach one clear value (kindness, courage, honesty, etc.)
  • ✓ 800-2000 words (5-10 minute read when narrated)
  • ✓ End with "That night, {childName}..." moral paragraph
  • ✓ Respectful of the tradition it represents
  • ✓ Original writing or retelling of folk/religious stories (no plagiarism)
  • ✓ Age-appropriate for kids 3-12
  • ✗ No violence, fear, or graphic content
  • ✗ No promotion of one tradition over another
