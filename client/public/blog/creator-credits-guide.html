What Makes a Great Story

Under 3 minutes — 250-400 words. Kids fall asleep during the story.

One clear lesson — courage, kindness, honesty. Pick one.

Use {childName} — the app personalizes it for each listener.

Conversational tone — write like you're telling a child on your lap.
