Email Notifications —
How We Keep You Updated
Without the Spam

We believe in meaningful communication. Here is exactly what emails we send, when we send them, and how you stay in control.

Our Email Philosophy

We built My Sleepy Tale for families. Families are busy. The last thing any parent needs is another platform flooding their inbox with noise.

So we made a promise to ourselves: every email we send must be useful, timely, and wanted. If an email does not help you enjoy the platform better, we do not send it.

We built a throttle layer into every single email endpoint. It checks category limits, respects your preferences, and silently skips anything that would be excessive. You will never get five "contribution submitted" emails in a row if your family is having a productive storytelling evening.

Our rule of thumb

If we would not want to receive this email ourselves at 10pm after putting kids to bed, we do not send it.

Throttling Rules

Every email passes through our throttle layer before it reaches your inbox. Here is how it works for each category:

1

Transactional — Always Sent

Welcome emails, payment confirmations, payment failures, and subscription changes are never throttled. These are critical messages you need to receive immediately, no matter what.

No limit
2

Activity — Max 5 per Day

Contributions submitted, episodes published, series shared, stories created, and rejections are capped at 5 emails per user per 24 hours. If you hit the limit, the system silently skips additional emails until the window resets.

5 per user per day
3

Marketing — Max 1 per Week

Weekly newsletters and blog announcements are limited to 1 marketing email per user per 7 days. Even if we publish three blog posts in one week, you will only receive the first notification. The system also checks your unsubscribe preference before attempting to send.

1 per user per week

What happens to skipped emails?

Nothing bad. The action still completes (your story is still submitted, your series is still shared). The only thing that does not happen is the email notification. You can always check the platform directly to see updates.

Every email endpoint runs through this flow. Transactional emails skip the throttle and unsubscribe checks entirely. Activity emails check the daily limit. Marketing emails check both the weekly limit and your unsubscribe preference.

Transactional Path

  • Category: transactional
  • Throttle check: skipped
  • Unsubscribe check: skipped
  • Result: always sent

Marketing Path

  • Category: marketing
  • Unsubscribe check: first
  • Throttle check: 1/week limit
  • Result: sent only if allowed

How to Unsubscribe

You can opt out of marketing emails (newsletters and blog announcements) at any time. There are two ways:

1

From the Settings Page

Go to Settings and toggle off newsletter notifications. Changes take effect immediately.

Recommended
2

From Any Marketing Email

Every newsletter and blog announcement email includes an Unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you are removed from the marketing list.

One click

What you will still receive

Unsubscribing only affects marketing emails (newsletters and announcements). You will still receive transactional emails (payment confirmations) and activity emails (when someone contributes to your series). These are essential platform notifications.

What We Never Do

This is just as important as what we do. Here are six commitments we make to every user:

We Never

  • Sell your email address to third parties
  • Send cold emails to people who never signed up
  • Send daily marketing blasts

We Also Never

  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use dark patterns to prevent unsubscribing
  • Re-subscribe you after you opt out

Your inbox is your personal space. We treat it with the same care we put into making bedtime stories for your children.
