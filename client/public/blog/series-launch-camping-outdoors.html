Camping & Outdoors — Bedtime Stories from the Wild

5 min read June 1, 2026 Series Launch

The sound of crickets. The smell of pine. A flashlight beam dancing across the ceiling of a tent. For millions of kids, camping is the most magical thing they will ever do. Now they can take that feeling to bed every night.

Camping & Outdoors is a 5-episode bedtime series that brings the warmth of a campfire, the wonder of the forest, and the closeness of family into your child's nighttime routine. Each episode takes place in the great outdoors and teaches something real about nature — and about growing up.

What Makes Outdoor Stories So Calming

Nature sounds are not just pleasant — they are physiologically calming. Research published in Scientific Reports found that natural soundscapes reduce the body's fight-or-flight response and increase rest-and-digest nervous system activity. When kids hear a story set by a crackling campfire beside a quiet lake, their bodies respond as though they are actually there.

This series uses that science intentionally. Gentle ambient sounds layer beneath the narration — soft wind, distant birdsong, the pop of a fire — creating an immersive experience that helps kids transition from the energy of the day to the stillness of sleep.

Episode Guide

  • The First Night Out — sleeping in a tent for the first time, facing the dark, and discovering that unfamiliar sounds are not scary once you understand them
  • The Trail to the Waterfall — hiking perseverance, reading trail markers, and the reward of reaching somewhere beautiful through effort
  • Campfire Friends — sharing food, telling stories, and how sitting in a circle makes everyone equal
  • The Rainy Morning — plans that change, puddle jumping, and learning that the best adventures are unplanned
  • Stars Above the Lake — constellations, reflections, and feeling small in the best possible way
Stories that bring the warmth of a campfire to bedtime

Values Kids Carry With Them

Each episode is built around a core value that parents care about: courage in the face of the unknown, persistence when things get hard, generosity around a shared meal, flexibility when plans change, and wonder at the world around us.

These are not preachy lessons. They are woven into the story so naturally that kids absorb them the way they absorb campfire warmth — gradually, without even noticing.

We listened to the camping series before our real camping trip. My daughter was so much braver in the tent because she had already "done it" in the story.

Perfect for Summer Bedtimes

When the sun sets late and kids resist sleep, this series gives them something to look forward to at bedtime. The outdoor setting matches the season, making the stories feel connected to their real lives. And at 8-10 minutes per episode, it fits neatly into even the most rushed bedtime routine.

Five episodes. Five nights of campfire magic. No bugs, no bears, no packing required.
