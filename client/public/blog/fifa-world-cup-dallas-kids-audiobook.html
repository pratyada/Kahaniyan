The World Cup Is in Texas

This summer, the biggest sporting event on the planet is coming to the Lone Star State. AT&T Stadium in Arlington is one of the largest FIFA World Cup 2026 venues — a colossal arena that seats over 80,000 fans, with a retractable roof that opens up to the Texas stars. For the first time in history, three countries are hosting together: the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Forty-eight teams. Billions of people watching. And your child can experience the magic from their pillow.

The FIFA World Cup 2026 is the largest World Cup ever held. More teams, more cities, more stories than any tournament before it. Dallas-Fort Worth is at the heart of it — and we have turned that pride into a bedtime series that makes the beautiful game gentle, magical, and educational for kids aged 3-10.

Why Dallas Kids Should Care About the World Cup

The World Cup is not just a football tournament. It is a classroom disguised as a celebration. Here is what your child picks up just by listening:

  • Geography — 48 countries are competing. That is 48 flags, 48 capitals, 48 places on the map your child will suddenly want to find. From Saudi Arabia to New Zealand, from Japan to Senegal — every match is a geography lesson. And it is all happening right here in their city.
  • Local pride — Texas has deep football roots. Clint Dempsey grew up in Nacogdoches, Texas and became one of the greatest American soccer players of all time. FC Dallas plays in Frisco, just north of the city, developing young talent every season. Your child is growing up in a soccer city — this series helps them feel it.
  • Languages — Our series is available in English, Spanish, French, and Hindi. Dallas is one of the most diverse cities in America. The Oak Cliff Hispanic community, the vibrant South Asian neighborhoods, the French-speaking families from West Africa — this series reflects the languages your child hears every day.
  • Values — Teamwork, perseverance, sportsmanship, handling defeat with grace, and celebrating others' victories. Football teaches all of it, and our stories make it stick.
Football connects children across every culture and continent

What's Inside the Series

Five episodes. Five bedtime journeys through the world's greatest sporting event — with a Texas lens. Each one runs 8-12 minutes and is narrated with warmth, wonder, and just enough detail to spark a child's imagination without keeping them awake.

  • Episode 1: How the World Cup Began — From the first tournament in Uruguay in 1930 to the record-breaking 2026 edition, this episode tells the story of how a simple idea — "let the whole world play" — became the biggest event in human history. Your child will learn why the trophy is gold, why the first World Cup had only 13 teams, and how the dream grew to 48.
  • Episode 2: Dallas — Our World Cup City — A bedtime tour of the places that make Dallas special this summer. AT&T Stadium in Arlington, where the roar of 80,000 fans shakes the ground. Fair Park, where fan festivals light up the night. The Trinity River trails where families gather. Oak Cliff, where soccer is a way of life and Spanish commentary fills the air. Frisco, where FC Dallas trains the next generation. This is your child's city — and this episode makes them proud of it.
  • Episode 3: The Beautiful Game — What is offside, really? Why do penalty shootouts make grown-ups cry? What happens in the goalkeeper's mind when the whole world is watching? This episode explains the rules and drama of football in a way that even a three-year-old can feel — and a ten-year-old will find fascinating.
  • Episode 4: Legends Who Changed the Game — Pelé dancing past defenders in black and white. Messi finally lifting the trophy in Qatar. Marta proving that the women's game is just as breathtaking. And Clint Dempsey from Texas — the kid from Nacogdoches who showed America that soccer is our sport too. These are the stories of players who turned football into art — told gently enough for bedtime.
  • Episode 5: Your World Cup Dream — Dallas Edition — This one is personal. Narrated with your child's name woven into the story, this episode imagines what it would feel like to walk onto the pitch at AT&T Stadium, hear 80,000 fans chanting under the Texas sky, and represent your country on the world's biggest stage. A bedtime story they will never forget.
