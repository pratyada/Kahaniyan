Voice Recording & Cloning Consent

Last updated: September 2026

My Sleepy Tale offers two very different voice features. We treat them differently because the law treats them differently, and we want you to understand exactly what happens with your family's voice.

This policy is provided in good faith and is being reviewed by legal counsel; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

Two features, one clear distinction

  • (a) Recording your own voice — a child (with parental consent) or an adult records their voice to create or narrate a story. This is record-and-playback. It is personal information we protect, but it is not a biometric — we do not build a voiceprint or template to identify the speaker.
  • (b) Cloning a family member's voice — you can optionally ask us to build a voiceprint so that stories can be narrated in a chosen family member's voice. A voiceprint is biometric and sensitive information, and we handle it under heightened safeguards.

Standard voices are the default

You never have to clone a voice. We offer high-quality standard (text-to-speech) narration voices as the default, less-invasive option. Voice cloning is strictly opt-in.

Consent for voice cloning

Before we create a voiceprint, we ask for express, standalone opt-in consent from the person whose voice is being cloned. If that person is a minor, a parent or guardian must give consent on their behalf. This consent is separate from agreeing to our general Terms — it is a deliberate, specific choice.

How voiceprints are stored & secured

Voiceprints are encrypted in transit and at rest, kept with access controls that limit who can reach them, and are not shared with third parties for their own purposes. We treat a voiceprint as among the most sensitive information we hold.

Retention

We keep a voiceprint only while you use the voice-cloning feature or as needed to provide it to you. When you stop using it or withdraw consent, we delete the voiceprint and its copies, including from backups on our standard backup cycle, within a reasonable period.

Delete my voice

You can withdraw consent and delete a voiceprint at any time through in-product controls or by emailing our Privacy Officer. Deleting a voiceprint stops future cloned narration and removes the underlying biometric data.

A note for Quebec

Quebec's Law 25 sets specific obligations for biometric information, including express consent and a declaration to the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI) before a biometric database is put into service. We are committed to meeting these obligations, and we are working through them with counsel before offering voice cloning to Quebec users. We do not claim to have completed the CAI declaration.
