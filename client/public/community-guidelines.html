Community Guidelines & Acceptable Use

Last updated: September 2026

My Sleepy Tale is a gentle, safe space for children and families. When you or your child create content — such as voice recordings and stories — these guidelines keep that space kind and safe for everyone. They form part of our Terms & Conditions.

This policy is provided in good faith and is being reviewed by legal counsel; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

What you agree to

  • Create only content that is appropriate for a children's product.
  • Upload or record only content you have the right to use.
  • Supervise your child's use of creation features.

What is not allowed

  • Content that is unsafe, violent, hateful, discriminatory, sexual, or otherwise inappropriate for children.
  • Content that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark, privacy, or other rights.
  • Anything that could exploit, endanger, or harm a child.
  • Personal information about others shared without their permission.
  • Attempts to disrupt, abuse, reverse-engineer, or misuse the Service.

Child-safety standards

Protecting children is our highest priority. Content that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits a child is strictly prohibited and will be removed. Where the law requires, we will report child-safety concerns to the appropriate authorities.

Moderation

Family content is private by default. We may review content that is reported to us or shared beyond a family, and we may remove content or suspend accounts that break these guidelines. We aim to act fairly and to explain our decisions where we can.

How to report a concern

If you see content or behaviour that worries you, email hello@mysleepytale.com (or privacy@mysleepytale.com for privacy concerns). Tell us what and where, and we'll look into it promptly.

Copyright takedown (notice-and-notice)

If you believe content on the Service infringes your copyright, please see our Copyright / IP & Takedown page, which explains how to send a notice under Canada's notice-and-notice regime.
