Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how My Sleepy Tale, a product of Musee Initialize Inc., uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you stay in control of them.

This policy is provided in good faith and is being reviewed by legal counsel; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

Non-essential cookies are off by default

We keep non-essential cookies off until you opt in. Essential cookies that are strictly necessary to run the Service (such as keeping you signed in) are always on because the Service cannot function without them. We do not use cookies to build behavioural advertising profiles, and never of children.

How to change your consent

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies, or change your mind at any time, using the cookie controls in the Service and your browser settings. Rejecting non-essential cookies will not stop you from using the core Service.

A note for Quebec (Law 25)

For our Quebec users, Quebec's Law 25 requires that technologies used to identify, locate, or profile a person be deactivated by default. We are committed to keeping non-essential trackers off by default and to offering clear choices to accept or reject them.
