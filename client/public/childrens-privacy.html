Children's Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

My Sleepy Tale is made for families, and protecting children's privacy is one of our deepest commitments. This page explains — in plain language for parents and guardians — how we care for your child's information.

This policy is provided in good faith and is being reviewed by legal counsel; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

The grown-up is always in charge

Only an adult (18+) can create and hold a My Sleepy Tale account. That account holder creates each child profile and is the consenting party for everything their child does on the Service. Children do not sign up on their own.

Verifiable parent/guardian consent

We are committed to obtaining verifiable consent from a parent or guardian before a child records their voice, adds content, or before any optional voice feature is used. We follow the guidance of the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada that, for younger children, consent must come from the parent or guardian. For families in Quebec, we treat parent/guardian consent for children under 14 as required.

Private by default

Your family's content is private to your family by default. Nothing your child creates is public unless you, the adult, deliberately choose to share it. There is no action you must take to keep it private — privacy is the starting point.

Your child's recordings are private

When your child records their own voice to make a story, that recording is for your family. It is played back to your child and the people you choose. Sharing is off by default, must be started by a parent, and can be turned off (revoked) by a parent at any time. We do not create public, discoverable links to children's content, and we do not use children's information to target advertising.

We collect as little as possible

We ask only for what a child profile needs to personalize stories — for example a first name or nickname and an age range. We don't ask children for information we don't need.

Voice recording vs. voice cloning

A child recording their own voice to make a story is record-and-playback — it is personal information we protect, but it is not a biometric, because we do not build a voiceprint to identify anyone. Our separate, optional feature that clones a family member's voice does involve a biometric voiceprint and is handled with heightened safeguards and separate consent. Please read Voice Recording & Cloning Consent.

Delete anytime

You can delete your child's recordings, a child profile, or your entire account whenever you like, and we will delete the associated information. See our Privacy Policy for retention and deletion details.

A note for U.S. families (COPPA)

For families in the United States, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requires verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13. We are committed to obtaining parental consent before a child provides information and to honouring parents' rights to review and delete their child's information. We are actively developing our approach to verifiable parental consent and do not yet claim a completed COPPA certification.
