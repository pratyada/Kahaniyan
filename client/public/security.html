Security & Data Protection

Last updated: September 2026

Keeping your family's information safe is a responsibility we take seriously. This page describes our commitment to protecting the data you trust us with.

This statement is provided in good faith and is being reviewed by legal counsel; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

Encryption

We protect personal information with encryption in transit (secure connections when data travels to and from the Service) and encryption at rest (data stored in an encrypted form). Voiceprints, when used for the optional voice-cloning feature, are treated as among the most sensitive data we hold.

Access controls

We limit access to personal information to those who need it to operate the Service, and we use authentication and internal controls to enforce that. We do not sell your family's data or give third parties access to it for their own purposes.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe, a certified PCI-DSS Level 1 service provider; we never store your full card details. Card data is handled directly by Stripe's secure systems, so sensitive payment information does not touch our own servers.

Where data is stored

We use Amazon Web Services (AWS) to store data, which may be located in the United States. See the cross-border section of our Privacy Policy for what that means for you.

Breach response commitment

Despite our safeguards, no system is perfectly secure. If a security incident creates a real risk of significant harm, we are committed to investigating promptly, notifying affected individuals and the appropriate regulators as required by law, and taking steps to contain and remediate the issue. A breach involving a voiceprint would be treated as presumptively reportable.

Report a security concern

If you believe you've found a vulnerability or a security issue, please email privacy@mysleepytale.com so we can address it quickly.
