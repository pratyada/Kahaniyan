Why Audio-Only Is the Right Choice

This is the most important design decision we made, and the one that makes the biggest difference for your child's sleep.

The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends no screens for at least one hour before bedtime. Blue light from tablets and phones suppresses melatonin production by up to 58%, making it harder for children to fall asleep and reducing sleep quality even after they do.

Most "bedtime story" apps ignore this. They show animated videos, bright images, and interactive elements — all of which wake your child up instead of putting them to sleep.

My Sleepy Tale is different. Tap play, put the phone face-down, and let the story fill the room. Your child closes their eyes and imagines the world. No light. No stimulation. Just a voice, a story, and the gentle drift toward dreams.

Whether you are a Hindu family in Toronto, a Muslim family in Houston, a Catholic family in Manila, or a mixed-heritage family in London — your child's stories are here.
