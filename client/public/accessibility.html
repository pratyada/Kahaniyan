Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 2026 · Last reviewed: September 2026

At My Sleepy Tale, we want every family to enjoy bedtime stories together. We are committed to making our Service usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

This statement is provided in good faith and is being reviewed by legal counsel; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

Our commitment & target standard

We aim to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 / 2.2 at Level AA and to align with EN 301 549. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we work to improve with each release.

Transcripts for audio stories

Because much of our content is audio, we are committed to providing text transcripts for our audio stories so they can be read as well as heard.

What we do

  • Design for keyboard navigation and screen-reader compatibility.
  • Aim for sufficient colour contrast and readable text.
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Support light and dark viewing.

Known limitations

We are still improving. Some newer or third-party features may not yet fully meet our target standard, and transcript coverage is expanding across our library. We are actively working to close these gaps.

Request an accommodation or report a barrier

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, or need content in an alternative format, please email hello@mysleepytale.com. Tell us the page or feature and the difficulty you experienced, and we will do our best to help and to fix it.
