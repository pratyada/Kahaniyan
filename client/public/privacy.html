Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

My Sleepy Tale is a product of Musee Initialize Inc., 373 Front Street West, Toronto, Ontario, Canada ("My Sleepy Tale", "we", "us", "our"). Protecting your family's privacy is central to everything we build. This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information we collect, why, how we protect it, and the choices you have. It reflects our commitment to Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws.

This policy is provided in good faith and is being reviewed by legal counsel; it is not a substitute for legal advice.

Our approach — the 10 privacy principles we build around

We aim to honour the ten fair information principles at the heart of Canadian privacy law:

  • 1. Accountability — We have a named Privacy Officer (below) who is responsible for our handling of personal information.
  • 2. Identifying purposes — We tell you why we collect information at or before the time we collect it.
  • 3. Consent — We collect, use, and disclose personal information with your knowledge and consent, and we ask for express opt-in consent for sensitive information such as voiceprints.
  • 4. Limiting collection — We collect only what we need to run the Service.
  • 5. Limiting use, disclosure & retention — We use information only for the purposes we described, and keep it only as long as needed.
  • 6. Accuracy — We keep information as accurate as is needed for its purpose.
  • 7. Safeguards — We protect information with encryption and access controls.
  • 8. Openness — We make our practices public through this policy.
  • 9. Individual access — You can ask to see and correct the information we hold about you.
  • 10. Challenging compliance — You can raise a concern with our Privacy Officer, and escalate to a regulator.

What we collect

  • Account information — the adult account holder's name, email, and login credentials. You must be 18+ to hold an account.
  • Child profiles — profiles you create for the children in your family (such as a first name or nickname and age range) so stories can be personalized. The adult account holder is the consenting party for every child profile.
  • Voice recordings — recordings a child (with your consent) or you make to create or narrate stories. These are personal information; a child recording their own voice is not a biometric.
  • Voiceprints for voice cloning — if, and only if, you choose our optional voice-cloning feature, we process a voiceprint of the family member whose voice is being cloned. A voiceprint is sensitive, biometric information handled under heightened safeguards. See our Voice Recording & Cloning Consent page.
  • Usage & device information — basic technical data (such as app interactions and device/browser type) to keep the Service working and improve it.
  • Payment information — handled by Stripe; we never receive or store your full card number. See our Security page.

Why we use it (purposes)

  • To create and personalize bedtime stories for your family.
  • To provide record-and-playback and, if you opt in, voice-cloning features.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To process subscriptions and respond to your requests.
  • To send you service messages and, only with your separate opt-in consent, occasional updates you can unsubscribe from at any time.

Consent

We rely on your consent to collect and use personal information. For sensitive information — including biometric voiceprints — we ask for express, standalone opt-in consent, and for voice cloning that consent must come from the person whose voice is being cloned (a parent or guardian if that person is a minor). You can withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits, by contacting our Privacy Officer or using in-product controls. For children's information, please also read our Children's Privacy page.

Where your data is stored — cross-border (US / AWS)

We store recordings and other data using Amazon Web Services (AWS). Your data, including voice recordings and any voiceprints, may be stored and processed on servers located in the United States. While it is there, it may be subject to access by U.S. courts, law enforcement, or national-security authorities under U.S. law. We use AWS under a data-processing agreement intended to provide a comparable level of protection, and we remain accountable for your information regardless of where it is processed. For our Quebec users, we are committed to assessing cross-border transfers as required under Quebec's Law 25.

Retention & deletion

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes described here or as required by law. You can delete recordings, child profiles, voiceprints, or your whole account at any time. When you delete something or withdraw consent for the voice-cloning feature, we delete the associated data — including from backups on our standard backup cycle — within a reasonable period.

Safeguards

We protect personal information with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls limiting who can reach your data, and internal policies. No system is perfectly secure, but we are committed to safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information — especially voiceprints. Learn more on our Security page.

Your access & correction rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you and your family, request a copy, and ask us to correct it. Contact our Privacy Officer and we will respond within the timelines required by law. Quebec users also have rights of de-indexing and portability, which we are committed to honouring.

Breach reporting

If a privacy breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we are committed to notifying affected individuals and the appropriate regulator (such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, and the Commission d'accès à l'information for Quebec) as required, and to keeping records of breaches. A breach involving a voiceprint would be treated as presumptively reportable.

Children's data

My Sleepy Tale is a family product and children's information receives special care. Please see our dedicated Children's Privacy page.
