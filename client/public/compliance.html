Welcome. My Sleepy Tale is built for families, and your family's privacy and safety guide the choices we make. This is one place to find every commitment we make about how we handle your information, keep children safe, and treat you fairly. We've written these pages in plain language and in good faith, as an honest picture of how we work.

These policies are provided in good faith and are being reviewed by legal counsel; they are not a substitute for legal advice.

How we handle your family's data — in short

  • Private by default — your family's stories and recordings are private unless you deliberately choose to share them.
  • Built to respect Canadian privacy law — we designed the Service around PIPEDA's principles and provincial privacy laws, with a named Privacy Officer you can reach.
  • Kids' voices are never cloned without express consent — a child recording their own voice is not a biometric; cloning a family member's voice is optional, opt-in, and consented separately.
  • Encrypted & access-controlled — data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and payments go through Stripe so we never store your card details.
  • You can delete anytime — recordings, profiles, voiceprints, or your whole account.
