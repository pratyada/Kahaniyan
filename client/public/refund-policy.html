Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

We want families to feel good about My Sleepy Tale. This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for our subscriptions. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions.

Cancel any time

You can cancel your subscription whenever you like — easily, from your account settings, with no phone calls and no hassle. When you cancel, you keep access to paid features until the end of your current billing period, and you will not be charged again. Your subscription renews automatically until you cancel; before it renews we aim to give you advance notice consistent with the consumer laws that apply to you.

Free trial

If your plan includes a free trial, you will not be charged during the trial. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing. If you don't cancel, your subscription begins and your payment method is charged when the trial ends.

Refunds

  • Renewals: Subscription fees are generally non-refundable for the current billing period once a charge has been made, but you can cancel to prevent future charges.
  • Accidental or duplicate charges: If you were charged in error, contact us within 14 days and we will make it right, typically with a full refund of the incorrect charge.
  • Something wrong on our end: If a technical problem meant you couldn't use a paid feature you were charged for, reach out — we'll help or refund fairly.
  • Local consumer rights: Nothing in this policy limits any refund or cancellation rights you may have under the consumer laws that apply to you. For example, Quebec consumers may have the right to a refund within a set period after an automatic renewal, and consumers in provinces such as British Columbia and Ontario have statutory cancellation and refund protections for subscription contracts. We are committed to honouring these rights.

How to cancel

Open your account, go to your plan/settings, and choose to cancel — or simply email us and we'll take care of it. Your cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via our payment provider (Stripe). Depending on your bank, it can take a few business days to appear.

Contact us

Questions, cancellations, or refund requests: email hello@mysleepytale.com and we'll respond promptly.

This policy is provided in good faith and is being reviewed by legal counsel; it is not a substitute for legal advice.
