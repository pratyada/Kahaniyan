Reporting infringement (notice-and-notice)

If you believe content on the Service infringes your copyright, send a notice to privacy@mysleepytale.com (or hello@mysleepytale.com) including:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Identification of the work you say is infringed.
  • The location (e.g., link or description) of the material on the Service.
  • Details of the claimed infringement.

Consistent with Canada's notice-and-notice regime under the Copyright Act, we will forward a complete and valid notice to the relevant user where appropriate, and we may remove or restrict content that appears to infringe.
