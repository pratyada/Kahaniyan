## Ongoing Social Strategy

### Daily Social Posts (pick 1-2)
- Story excerpt or moral of the day
- Parenting tip related to bedtime
- Behind-the-scenes of app development
- User testimonial or review
- "Did you know?" cultural fact from a story tradition
- Poll or question to drive engagement

### Platforms (Priority Order)
1. **Instagram** — Visual story cards, reels about bedtime routines
2. **Twitter/X** — Parenting tips, threads about cultural stories
3. **Facebook Groups** — Share in Indian parenting, faith-based, homeschool groups
4. **Pinterest** — Story illustrations, bedtime routine infographics
5. **LinkedIn** — Founder journey, edtech insights

### Content Repurposing
- Every blog post becomes: 1 social post + 1 story card + 1 short video script
- Every 5 posts become: 1 email newsletter
- Top posts become: YouTube shorts / Instagram reels
